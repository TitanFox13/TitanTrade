## 4. The discipline — how every result in this project was produced

Hypotheses in the script docstring before anything runs; marginal effects before leaderboards; a grid finds a family, never a winner. Walk the *selection* forward both ways (decade split 2022-01-01; century split 1975 for books, 1990 for universe tests; four quarter-century eras), and report through 2025-12-31 beside the full period because 2026 dominates every momentum number. Any single-name universe needs the point-in-time membership mask. Judge against SPY **and** the equal-weight hold of the universe; the 11 sector ETFs are the hindsight-free floor. De-risked capital earns 0 % in the engine (a deliberate bias against de-risking rules; `carry.py` is the honest-expectation line with real bill rates; Decision 080 priced the destinations). Costs 0.05 % a side, 1 %/yr borrow on shorts, price a short as the broker would. **Every state-, label- or timing-based idea gets a placebo** with the same count and shape (63-session block shuffles, 20–50 draws); a cell with one to three episodes is an anecdote. Anything that survives gets the luck meter (`luck.py`: deflated Sharpe, PBO, block bootstrap).

Lessons of 2026-10-29/30 (`docs/insights.md`): the vehicle is not the premium (twelve quality funds, none captured what the French portfolios show); equal weight gives a premium back (our own quality picks); point in time means first reported (SEC XBRL, earliest filing wins); a revised series knows the future (the NFCI passed, its first-release vintages did not); the fall is the fact, the fear gauge is the state (VIX); a hedge that worked once is an episode (gold = 1973–74); bonds rally when the book steps aside (why a short Treasury beats bills as the parking place). Data: every Alpaca file in `data/param_search/alpaca_hist/` is dividend-adjusted (`adjustment=all`); never mix a split-only fetch; `historical_long/SPY.json` ends 2026-10-12 on purpose.
