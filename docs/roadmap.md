# TitanTrade Roadmap

*Written 2026-10-29 after the first day of the mechanical book's second design (Decision 070). A living document: every item here is either done, ready to test with data in hand, waiting on the operator, waiting on live time, or closed with the evidence named. Results go to `docs/decisions.md`; the state of each item is updated here when it moves.*

**Status marks**: ✅ done · 🔬 ready to test (data in hand, pre-register and run) · 🔑 needs the operator (a key, a sign-up, a decision, money) · ⏳ needs live time · 🚫 closed — not to be re-proposed without the new evidence named in the row · 💡 idea, not yet designed.

**The standard every test meets** (Decision 062 → 068): hypotheses in the script docstring before the first run; hindsight-free universes (the sector ETFs, the French century; point-in-time membership for any single-name list); in-sample / out-of-sample halves both ways and the four quarter-centuries; judged against SPY *and* the equal-weight hold of the universe; costs charged; every state-, label- or timing-based idea against a placebo with the same count and shape; a grid finds a family, never a cell; the luck meter (deflated Sharpe, bootstrap band) on anything that survives. Nothing goes live from research: a Decision with the numbers, then the operator's go.

**The data rule** (operator, 2026-10-30): before any test, look for a *free* source of the data it needs, and look hard; what is free is used. What exists only as paid data is **not bought one test at a time**: the item moves to the back of the roadmap into the "needs paid data" group (Track H, last table), and when enough items sit there the data is bought once, from one source if one covers them all, for one month, and cancelled.

**The book today** (Decision 070; the 2 % working reserve from Decision 090, live since 2026-11-03): 60 % SPMO held · 40 % dip rule on the 11 sector ETFs (3 × 10 % slots, −12 % / +20 % / 60 trading days) with SPY as the residual · two-signal insurance ×0.5 · shock trigger ×0.5 · exposure = min · the hand-picked 15 names as a shadow list, tracked not traded · eight gates · broker-side GTC stops · a 2 % working cash reserve (the core legs' buys net of pending brackets). Hindsight-free expectation, **restated 2026-11-03 for the 2 % reserve (Decision 090; the 5 % book read +1.0, Decision 081)**: about +1.5 pp/yr vs SPY in a decade like the last (first half +0.2, second +2.8; worst relative year −11.0; worst drawdown −19.7 % vs −34 %; Sharpe 1.12 vs 0.89) — the blend's "+1.4, both halves positive" was 0.3 the reserve the live book holds on the whole book and 0.1 the daily free rebalancing the executor does not do; +3.6 over a century with every quarter-century ahead (a blend of research series — a frictionless momentum decile, the SMA-only insurance — labelled as such). The return edge is a century claim; the drawdown behaviour is the better-evidenced half.
